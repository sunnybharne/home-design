        Your YouTube corner

        The lower bedroom is proposed as a studio / guest room: compact desk, closed daybed and an oak bookcase backdrop. This is a flexible room-use suggestion.

        • Window light from the side, not directly behind your face. Use linen curtains to soften it.
        • Warm background light, books, ceramics and simple art. Keep the host away from the back wall where space allows.
        • The rug and curtains may soften reflections. They do not soundproof the room; test traffic noise, echo and your microphone.
        • The 16:9 view is a composition guide, not a validated camera/lens or skin-tone test. Recording lights are generic equipment suggestions.
        • Check drawer travel, pulled-out chairs, the open daybed and your delivery route on site.
