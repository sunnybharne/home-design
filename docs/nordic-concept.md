# Soft Nordic furnishing concept

## Direction

Warm pale wood, off-white fabric, linen curtains, muted olive and clay accents. Simple shapes, visible grain, soft cushions and a few books, plants and ceramics. Keep the supplied beige kitchen, white walls and selected bathroom finishes.

The lower bedroom is proposed as a guest room / YouTube studio. It is still a bedroom, not a claim that its approved use has changed.

## Rendering changes

- Physically based materials, original procedural wood/linen/stone textures and subtle surface relief.
- Rounded cushion edges, upholstery piping, bed linen folds, tapered chair/table legs and drawer reveals.
- Detailed kitchen fronts, hob, oven, tap and plinth; simplified sanitary fixtures.
- Curtains, original wall art, plants, books, ceramics and paper pendants.
- Environment reflections, soft window-area lights, cached shadow maps and optional screen-space contact shadows.
- A working entrance mirror in desktop mode. The lighter mobile mode uses a simpler reflection material.
- Less extreme wide-angle room views, plus daylight, evening and recording light moods.
- A centred 16:9 guide and watermarked room-image export.
- Static views stop rendering until the view or lighting changes. Smooth mode skips contact-shadow post-processing.

These are original approximations, **not IKEA photos or official IKEA 3D assets**. Laptop, microphone, recording equipment, art, ceramics, plants and bedding are generic styling. Texture generation and geometry stay in the app; no copied images or owner PDFs are shipped.

## Filming idea

Use side window light rather than a bright window directly behind the host. Curtains soften the window visually. A warm bookcase light, books and simple art provide background depth. The recording preset is a background-composition study; it has no human figure or calibrated camera/lens model.

Rugs and curtains may reduce some room reflections, but do not soundproof the apartment. Check echo, outside noise, microphone position, colour temperature and flicker in the actual room. Generic recording lights are suggestions, not electrical installation instructions.

## Limits and checks

- This is a real-time design study, not a photograph, a path-traced render or a measured digital twin.
- Lighting is illustrative: no real sun orientation, lux, daylight, colour-match or acoustic claim.
- Chairs are tucked in and the daybed is closed. Opening/pull-out and delivery clearances are not validated.
- Core furniture footprints are shared by the plan, models and navigation. Small decorative overhangs are simplified.
- Tests cover product references, mesh validity/budget, furniture overlap, camera positions, recording crop and room reachability.
- The floor plan, living, island and upper-cupboard views were checked in the browser. Upper storage was checked in daylight and evening moods. Export and full mobile interaction checks remain open.
